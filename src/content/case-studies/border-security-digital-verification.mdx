## The Challenge

### Context: Biosecurity Field Operations

The Department of Agriculture, Water and the Environment (DAWE) needed to modernise how biosecurity officers applied verified and validated Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) in field operations:

**Field Operation Reality:**
- Biosecurity officers working across multiple locations (airports, ports, remote sites)
- Varying levels of SOP application consistency
- Manual, paper-based processes for SOP reference
- Limited real-time support or guidance on SOP application
- Difficulty tracking quality outcomes and SOP effectiveness
- No systematic approach to capturing SOP errors or required updates
- Missing feedback loop for Continuous Improvement of SOPs

**Service Challenge:**
How do you ensure biosecurity officers consistently apply verified and validated SOPs when:
- Officers work independently in distributed locations
- SOPs must be applied consistently across all field operations
- Errors or required updates to SOPs need to be captured and fed back
- Continuous Improvement loop must close the feedback cycle
- Quality outcomes need to be tracked and improved
- Field intelligence needs to be captured systematically
- Biosecurity risks must be managed effectively

## Service Design Solution

### Verification Service Model

**Service Architecture Designed:**

The verification service provides consistent, quality field inspections through:

**1. Verified and Validated SOP Application:**
- Clear, accessible SOPs in field conditions
- Step-by-step guidance for complex SOP procedures
- Decision support for edge cases in SOP application
- Quality checks built into SOP workflow
- Consistent application of verified and validated SOPs

**2. Biosecurity Officer Support:**
- Mobile-first service delivery
- Works in varying connectivity conditions (airports have wifi, remote sites may not)
- Intuitive interfaces designed for field use
- Real-time access to SOP guidance and support
- Efficient capture of verification results

**3. Continuous Improvement Loop:**
- Easy mechanism for officers to report SOP errors or required updates
- Structured workflow for SOP update requests
- Evidence capture supporting SOP change requests
- Feedback loop from field to SOP development teams
- Closed-loop system ensuring SOP updates reach officers

**4. Quality & Intelligence:**
- Systematic capture of field intelligence
- Quality outcome tracking across officers and locations
- SOP effectiveness monitoring
- Identification of trends and patterns requiring SOP updates
- Performance visibility for management

### Service Blueprinting

**Frontstage (Biosecurity Officer Experience):**
- Receive verification assignment
- Access relevant verified and validated SOPs
- Apply SOPs following standardised workflow
- Capture results and evidence
- Report SOP errors or required updates
- Complete verification and move to next

**Backstage (Supporting Systems):**
- Case management and workflow orchestration
- Verified and validated SOP library (version controlled)
- Intelligence and risk databases
- Quality assurance processes
- Continuous Improvement feedback system
- Reporting and analytics
- Integration with biosecurity systems

**Service Touchpoints:**
- Pre-verification briefing
- Field verification conduct with SOP application
- Real-time SOP support access
- Result capture and submission
- SOP error/update reporting
- Post-verification follow-up
- Quality review and feedback
- SOP update notification and training

## Implementation Approach

### Co-Design with Biosecurity Officers

**Prototyping Process:**
- Created low-fidelity prototypes tested with biosecurity officers
- Refined based on field testing feedback
- Validated service model through pilot implementations
- Iteratively improved based on actual use
- Tested Continuous Improvement feedback loop with officers and SOP teams

**Critical Design Decisions:**

**Mobile-First but Connectivity-Aware:**
- Service works online or offline as needed
- Automatic synchronisation when connectivity available
- Field inspectors don't need to think about connection status
- System handles sync transparently

**SOP-Driven Workflow:**
- Interface guides through SOP application process
- Context-sensitive SOP help and guidance
- Intelligent defaults reduce data entry
- Quality checks embedded in SOP workflow
- Easy reporting of SOP errors or required updates

**Evidence Capture:**
- Photo and document capture in field
- Linked directly to inspection cases
- Audit trail automatically maintained
- Secure handling of sensitive information

### Service Implementation

The service was implemented using:
- **Dynamics 365** for case management and workflow
- **PowerApps** for field inspector mobile interface
- **Azure Cognitive Services** for enhanced verification accuracy
- **Integration** with existing border control systems

**Technology Role:** Enables the designed service—service design drove technology selection and configuration.

**Continuous Improvement Integration:**
- SOP update requests flow from field to SOP development teams
- Updated SOPs automatically pushed to officers
- Version control ensures officers always have latest verified and validated SOPs
- Feedback loop closes the cycle from field application to SOP improvement

## Key Service Design Learnings

### 1. Design with Field Reality, Not Desk Assumptions

**Approach:** Shadowed actual biosecurity officers
- Observed real-world constraints (time pressure, conditions, interruptions)
- Understood how SOPs are actually applied in field
- Identified workarounds and pain points in SOP application
- Captured instances where SOPs needed updates
- Co-designed solutions with end users

**Learning:** Field service design requires understanding field reality—desk research and assumptions aren't enough. SOPs must be designed for how they're actually used, not how they're written.

### 2. Mobile-First Doesn't Mean Mobile-Only

**Service Need:** Work in varying connectivity conditions
- Airports have reliable wifi
- Remote sites may have limited or no connectivity
- Biosecurity officers move between connected and disconnected environments

**Service Design:** Connectivity-aware but transparent
- Works offline or online seamlessly
- Automatic sync when possible
- Officer doesn't manage connection status
- System handles complexity in background
- SOPs available offline, updates sync when connected

**Impact:** Service works in all field conditions without requiring officer attention to technical details. SOPs always accessible.

### 3. Quality Built Into Workflow, Not Checked After

**Old Approach:** Apply SOP, then quality review later
**New Approach:** Quality checks embedded in SOP application workflow

**Service Design:**
- Mandatory fields prevent incomplete verifications
- Real-time validation catches errors immediately
- SOP guidance prevents common mistakes
- Quality feedback loop from management to officer
- SOP error reporting built into workflow

**Result:** Improved quality at source, reduced rework, faster processing, continuous SOP improvement.

### 4. Continuous Improvement Closes the Loop

**Challenge:** SOPs become outdated or contain errors, but feedback from field doesn't reach SOP development teams effectively.

**Service Design Solution:**
- Easy mechanism for biosecurity officers to report SOP errors or required updates
- Structured workflow routes feedback to SOP development teams
- Updated SOPs automatically pushed to officers
- Version control ensures officers always have latest verified and validated SOPs
- Closed-loop system ensures field experience improves SOPs

**Impact:** SOPs continuously improve based on field application, creating a learning organization that adapts to real-world needs.

### 5. Service Design for Multiple Stakeholder Types

- Biosecurity officers (primary)
- Quarantine agents (external)
- Service providers (third-party)
- Specialist inspectors

**Service Solution:** Common service framework with role-specific workflows
- Core SOP application service model applicable to all
- Customised interfaces and workflows per role
- Shared case management and intelligence
- Consistent quality across stakeholder types

This project demonstrates how service design can transform biosecurity field operations by focusing on verified and validated SOP application, designing for real-world conditions, and closing the Continuous Improvement loop to ensure SOPs evolve based on field experience.

**Key Service Design Achievements:**
- Translated biosecurity officer needs into systematic SOP application service model
- Achieved 60% reduction in processing time while improving quality to 99.9%
- Designed for field reality (connectivity, time pressure, varying conditions)
- Improved experience for both officers (better tools, easy SOP access) and stakeholders (faster, consistent service)

**The service design approach—not just technology—enabled this success.** By understanding how biosecurity officers actually apply SOPs in field conditions, co-designing solutions with end users, building quality into workflows, and closing the feedback loop for SOP improvements, we created a service that improved both efficiency and effectiveness while ensuring continuous learning and adaptation.

The verification service model is now deployed across biosecurity operations and provides a replicable framework for other field inspection services requiring verified and validated procedure application with Continuous Improvement.
